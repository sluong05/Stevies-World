## Portion assumptions

The complete broth and sauce are included; **optional eggs and green onion garnish are excluded**. Ready-to-use udon weights are not dry-noodle weights.
- Timings (35 and 30 minutes) are estimates. Noodle package cooking times take precedence over the handwritten approximate 5 minutes.

## Optional egg

One large egg (50 g edible weight) adds approximately **72 kcal, 6.3 g protein, 0.4 g carbs and 4.8 g fat**, based on [USDA raw whole-egg composition](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/food-details/171287/nutrients). At the referenced [Walmart dozen price of $1.67](https://www.walmart.com/c/kp/eggs-12-count-great-value), one egg costs about **$0.14**. One optional egg per udon bowl therefore brings it to roughly **702 kcal, 38.9 g protein and $4.95 per bowl**, before optional green onions. The food image includes optional egg; it does not represent the base calculation.
