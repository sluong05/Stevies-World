# Recipe estimate methodology

Source review: October 13, 2026. Currency: USD. These are approximate recipe calculations and grocery budgeting examples, not individualized nutrition advice or live store quotes.

## Calculation rules

Nutrition is per original serving. Sum each ingredient’s `grams × nutrient per 100 g ÷ 100`, then divide by the recipe’s original serving count. The ingredient tables show full-recipe contributions before rounding; the website rounds energy to the nearest 10 kcal and macros to the nearest gram. Carbohydrates include fiber, so calories need not equal exactly `4 × protein + 4 × carbs + 9 × fat`. All listed oil/sauce is treated as consumed. Cooking losses and brand differences are not modeled. Tap water contributes no macros or material ingredient cost. Salt and small amounts of pepper have negligible macros here. Optional toppings and serving extras are excluded.

Costs allocate only the portion of each package used; edible produce portions are charged at their stated unit cost, and whole-item ingredients such as an avocado, lemon, or can include their unavoidable peel/pit/drained liquid. Cost allowances are rounded to cents at the ingredient level and summed, then divided by servings for the UI. Taxes, delivery, equipment, energy, and spoilage are excluded. Buying all packages for the first time costs more. Listed online prices are examples surfaced during research, sometimes indexed or promotional, without a fixed store/location; they are not current local quotes. Other costs are explicitly labeled assumptions.

Portion weights use USDA measures except the stated chosen sizes (150 g avocado flesh, 45 g large bread slice, and 40 g packed spinach/cup).

Broth uses the [Pacific Foods manufacturer label](https://www.pacificfoods.com/products/pacific-foods/organic-low-sodium-vegetable-broth/): per 240 mL, 10 kcal, 0 g protein, 3 g carbohydrate, and 0 g fat. Unseasoned rice vinegar uses a negligible distilled-vinegar nutrient proxy; whole-grain bread uses whole-wheat bread; red lentils use generic dry lentils; smoked paprika uses generic paprika. These are explicitly approximate, not laboratory analysis of the cooked meals.

## Alternative ingredients

The oats default is dairy milk; fortified unsweetened soy milk remains a valid recipe option but its own label must be used for revised macros and costs. Optional maple syrup, pita, sesame seeds, bread alongside soup, fruit/beans alongside eggs, and rice alongside the chickpea bowl are excluded. The displayed figures do not automatically recompute for substitutions. Use your product labels and actual quantities for a closer estimate.
